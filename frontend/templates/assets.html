Indian Income Tax Depreciation Rule (Section 32)

Depreciation under corporate taxation in India is allowed on block of assets using WDV method. Standard rates: Computers & Software: 40%, General Plant & Machinery: 15%, Office Furniture & Fittings: 10%, Buildings: 10%.
