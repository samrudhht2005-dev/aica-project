Section 80JJAA Tax Planning Opportunity

Indian Income Tax allows a 30% deduction of the salary cost of new additional employees for 3 consecutive years if:
• Gross salary is <= Rs. 25,000/month.
• Employee is employed for >= 240 days (or 150 days in apparel/leather).
• Employee participates in the EPF scheme.
